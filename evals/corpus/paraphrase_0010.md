# The meaning of the study of what happens after death in various cultural traditions

Every human culture has had to confront the fact that people stop living and have devised some story about what happens next. These stories vary enormously: reincarnation, ancestor realms, heavenly gardens, absorption into a cosmic whole, or simple extinction.

Despite the diversity, certain patterns recur. There is often a journey, a judgement, a transformation. The common thread is that death is rarely treated as a simple end. It is a passage, a door, a change of state. The stories we tell about it reveal more about how we live than about what awaits.
