# Phase distill findings

The settings pages carry five sources of complexity: a full-page push/pop navigation for only four sections (each `teardown()` + `view.innerHTML` swap with its own back-button boilerplate), an accent-dot uppercase eyebrow (`.shelf`) repeated before every group, over-rounded 20px inputs with a phantom 450 font-weight and -0.08px tracking that the bundled font cannot render, zero focus styling on any form control, and the Trash page embedding a full deleted-items list inside a settings form. The distill is: collapse the four sub-pages into a single tabbed or accordion surface, strip the shelf eyebrow to a quiet label, align inputs to the DESIGN.md 8px/1px/lavender-focus spec, and move the trash list behind its own progressive-disclosure entry point.

- [ ] **D.1 [AGENT]** Replace the four full-page settings sub-pages with a single tabbed surface.
  Anchor: `showSettings()` at `museum.html:6755` (nav hub with 4 `settings-nav-row` buttons), and `showSettingsAI()` at `:6801`, `showSettingsFeatures()` at `:7053`, `showSettingsStorage()` at `:7131`, `showSettingsTrash()` at `:7181` (each calls `teardown()` + `view.innerHTML` swap + its own back button).
  Each sub-page is a full DOM replacement with a hand-built back button (`settingsBack` at `:6808`, `:7059`, `:7131`, `:7181`) that calls `showSettings()` to return. That is push/pop stack navigation for four sections - heavier than the content warrants. The distill reference: "Linear flow: Replace complex grids with simple vertical flow" and "Remove steps." The product register: "Standard navigation patterns: top bar + side nav, breadcrumbs, tabs."
  Replace with a tab bar (4 tabs: AI, Features, Storage, Trash) that swaps only the form content below it, not the whole view. One `view.innerHTML`, one back button in the app header (the existing `.round` gear at `:3305` already returns to the wall), no per-page `settingsBack` button. The `currentSettingsView` re-render mechanism (`:6978`) still works: it re-renders the current tab, not the whole page.
  Verify: entering settings shows 4 tabs and the AI form in one render; switching tabs does not call `teardown()` or flash a `state` loading state; no `settingsBack` button appears.

- [ ] **D.5 [AGENT]** Reduce `.group` padding from 48px to 24px and remove the hard-coded max-width.
  Anchor: `.group` at `museum.html:2366` (`padding: var(--sp-6)` = 48px; `max-width: 860px` at `:2373`).
  DESIGN.md section 4: "Card interior padding is `lg` 24px on feature cards." The current 48px is double the spec. Combined with `--sp-4` (20px) between fields, the card interiors are very airy - more than the design system calls for. The `max-width: 860px` is a hard-coded pixel value, not a token, and is narrower than the artifact view's 930px (`--bodygrid` at `:1537`), creating an inconsistent content width. The distill reference: "Consistent spacing: Use one spacing scale, remove arbitrary gaps."
  Set padding to `var(--sp-lg)` (24px) and either remove `max-width` (let the parent container govern width) or repoint it to the same `max-width` the rest of the app uses. The field-separator rule (`.group > .field + .field` at `:2374`, `margin-top: var(--sp-4)` = 20px) can stay or tighten to `--sp-md` (16px) per DESIGN.md's 16px rhythm.
  Verify: settings group cards have 24px interior padding; no hard-coded `860px` width remains; the content width is consistent with the rest of the app.

- [ ] **D.6 [AGENT]** Move the trash item list out of the settings form and behind a progressive-disclosure entry point.

  Verify: the Trash settings tab shows the retention input and an "Empty trash" button with a count, not the full item list; the item list is behind a separate entry point.
